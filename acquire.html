Common Questions

Questions We Hear Most Often

Will my clients know I'm selling?

Not until the deal closes and you choose to tell them. The entire process is confidential. We sign an NDA before any financial details are shared, and we never contact your clients during the process.

What happens to my staff?

We evaluate staffing on a deal-by-deal basis. We're not able to make universal commitments here, but your staff's institutional knowledge and relationships are assets we value. We'll discuss this openly in early conversations.

How long does the process take?

Most owners go from first conversation to a signed letter of intent in 4–8 weeks. The timeline is driven by you — we don't rush, and we don't disappear for weeks at a time.

Do I have to stay on after closing?

Yes — we require a minimum two-year consulting or salary arrangement post-close. This is non-negotiable because it's what protects your clients and ensures a smooth transition. Most owners find they appreciate having the structure.

What if I'm just exploring — not ready to sell?

That's exactly when to reach out. Understanding what your business is worth and what a deal could look like costs you nothing. Many of our best conversations start with "I'm just curious." There is zero pressure to move forward.

How is the purchase price determined?

We look at your trailing 12-month revenue, client retention rate, specialty mix, and operational complexity. We walk you through our valuation methodology openly — you'll never receive an offer without understanding exactly how we got there.
