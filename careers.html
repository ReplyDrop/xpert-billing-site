Qualifications

What We Look For

  • 2+ years of medical billing experience — hands-on claims submission, denial management, and AR follow-up in a real practice or billing company setting.
  • EHR/PMS proficiency — experience in one or more systems (eClinicalWorks, Kareo, AdvancedMD, Athena, Office Ally, or similar). We work in the client's existing system.
  • Strong knowledge of Medicare & Medicaid billing rules — including modifier usage, bundling edits, and payer-specific requirements.
  • Specialty experience a plus — particularly behavioral health, physical therapy, dermatology, orthopedics, or primary care.
  • HIPAA compliance knowledge — understanding of PHI handling requirements in a remote or in-office environment.
  • Self-directed and detail-oriented — our billers own their accounts. You'll need to stay on top of your AR, payer updates, and denial trends without being micromanaged.
