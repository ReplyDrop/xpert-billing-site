US-Based vs. Offshore Billing Teams: What It Actually Means

A lot of medical billing companies now route day-to-day claims work through overseas teams to offer lower headline pricing. That's not automatically a bad thing — but it changes a few things worth understanding before you switch, especially for a NY practice navigating payers like EmblemHealth, Fidelis Care, and eMedNY.

US-Based TeamOffshore / Outsourced Team
Payer familiarityDirect, ongoing experience with NY-specific payer rules, eMedNY, and Medicaid managed care nuancesOften generalized across states; state-specific rule changes can take longer to reach the team handling your account
Point of contactOne dedicated biller who knows your accountFrequently a shared queue or rotating rep
Time zone alignmentSame business hours as your practice — same-day answersTime zone gaps can delay responses to urgent denial or authorization issues
In-person availabilityAvailable for local practices when it's usefulRarely an option
Typical pricingOften 3–7% of collectionsSometimes 2–3%, reflecting lower labor cost

Lower pricing isn't inherently a red flag — but if a practice's clean claim rate or denial resolution is doing the real work of "affordability," a lower percentage on paper can still mean less money collected in practice. The rate you're quoted is only half the equation; the other half is how much of your revenue actually gets collected.

Questions Worth Asking Any Billing Company Before You Sign

Where are the people actually working my claims located?

Ask directly — not "where are you headquartered," but where the billers touching your account day-to-day are based.

Will I have one dedicated contact, or a shared support queue?

A named biller who knows your practice's history tends to catch recurring denial patterns a rotating queue misses.

How quickly do you respond to a denial or urgent authorization issue?

Ask for a specific number — hours, not "we prioritize denials." Time zone gaps show up here first.

What happens to my data and workflow if I leave?

A billing partner working inside your existing EHR (rather than migrating you to their platform) makes an eventual exit — if you ever need one — far less disruptive.

Can I see your BAA and HIPAA safeguards before I commit?

Any legitimate billing company should be able to walk you through this without hesitation.
